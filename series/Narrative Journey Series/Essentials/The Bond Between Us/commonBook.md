<CommunityCovenant>
> We prize the family as a bedrock of faith formation,  
> we cherish marriage as a most holy and most sacred union,  
> we value parents as frontline shepherds to disciple their children,  
> we treasure friends as our own souls,  
> we engage society as godly citizens with redemptive purpose, and  
> we embrace our lifework with a God-centered focus.  
>
> We confess the church as Christ's family, a family called to:  
> treat other members with genuine care,  
> steward our possessions for the common good,  
> honor appointed leaders and participate in Christ's worldwide body,  
> engage the domains of the world with the truth of the gospel, and  
> represent Christ to all those who are far and near.
